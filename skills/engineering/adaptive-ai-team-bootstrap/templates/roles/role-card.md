# Role Card

Template-Version: adaptive-ai-team-bootstrap@0.1.0

## Role

Name the role and keep it narrow enough to own clear outputs.

## Mission

Describe the outcome this role protects.

## Activation Triggers

- List signals that should activate this role.

## Inputs

- List required context, artifacts, or approvals.

## Outputs

- List concrete artifacts or decisions this role produces.

## Authority

- State whether the role can advise, execute, review, block, or escalate.

## Tools Allowed

- List tools or say `Project-approved tools only.`

## Cannot Do

- List actions outside this role's authority.

## Verification Duties

- List evidence this role must produce or check.

## Escalation Path

- State who receives unresolved issues.

## Retirement Criteria

- State when this role should be removed or merged.
