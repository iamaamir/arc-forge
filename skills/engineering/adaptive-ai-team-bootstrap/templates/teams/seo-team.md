# SEO Team Charter

Template-Version: adaptive-ai-team-bootstrap@0.1.0

## Team Name

SEO Team

## Mission

Protect crawlability, metadata, content structure, canonical URLs, performance, and indexability.

## Scope

- Crawlability, metadata, structured content, canonical URLs, redirects, sitemap, and indexability concerns.
- Public-page performance, accessibility, and content structure that affect search visibility.

## Non-Scope

- Private application workflows, unrelated backend features, and non-public content strategy.
- Brand, campaign, or copy decisions that do not affect technical SEO or indexability.

## Owner

- Assigned by Human Owner during approval.

## Owned Domains Or Files

- Public route metadata, canonical URL policy, sitemap and robots configuration concepts.
- Structured data, content hierarchy, redirect behavior, and crawl/render verification notes.
- Search-facing performance and accessibility checklists.

## Roles

- Team owner: Assigned by Human Owner during approval.
- Specialist reviewer: Domain specialist responsible for quality gates.
- Implementer liaison: Implementer assigned through the active task brief.

## Quality Gates

- Metadata checked.
- Crawl/render implications reviewed.
- Performance and accessibility impact considered.
- Public-facing content changes reviewed against product intent.

## Dependencies

- Product Lead, Staff Engineer, Implementer, Reviewer, QA/Verification, and Docs/Memory Steward.
- Content, design, analytics, platform, and performance teams when public surface changes overlap.

## Escalation Rules

- Escalate high-risk indexability, canonical, migration, or public-content conflicts to Human Owner.
- Escalate rendering or architecture trade-offs that affect crawlability to Staff Engineer.

## Scaling Trigger

- Activate when evidence shows repeated public-page changes, SEO regressions, site migrations, or search-impacting feature work.

## Retirement Criteria

- Collapse or deactivate when search-impacting work is complete, verification is documented, and ownership returns to baseline roles.
