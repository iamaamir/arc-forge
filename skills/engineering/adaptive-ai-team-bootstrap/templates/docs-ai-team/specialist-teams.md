# Specialist Teams

Template-Version: adaptive-ai-team-bootstrap@0.1.0

## Creation Requirements

No specialist team should exist without team name, mission, scope, non-scope, owner, owned domains, roles, quality gates, dependencies, escalation rules, scaling trigger, and retirement criteria.

## Scaling Triggers

- Recurring domain-specific work.
- Repeated review failures.
- Security, compliance, or data sensitivity.
- Ownership ambiguity.
- Cross-team dependency load.
- Domain-specific quality gates.

## De-Scaling Triggers

- Domain work becomes rare.
- Coordination overhead exceeds value.
- Role boundaries overlap repeatedly.
- Team has no active responsibilities.
