# Scaling Model

Start lean. Add specialist roles or teams only when evidence shows they are useful.

## Scaling Triggers

- recurring domain-specific work
- repeated review failures in one domain
- security, compliance, or data sensitivity
- growing backlog or ownership ambiguity
- cross-team dependency load
- high-risk architecture or release surface
- need for domain-specific quality gates

## De-Scaling Triggers

- domain work becomes rare
- team produces more coordination overhead than value
- role boundaries overlap repeatedly
- specialist team has no clear owner, mission, or active responsibilities

## Specialist Team Requirements

No specialist team should exist without:

- mission
- scope
- non-scope
- owner
- owned domains or files
- quality gates
- dependencies
- escalation rules
- retirement criteria
